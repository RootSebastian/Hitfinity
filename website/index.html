And everything else
💾

Offline-first, forever

Everything lives in a local SQLite database via Drift. No accounts, no sign-ups, no servers, no tracking. Your training history belongs to you and works on a plane.

🌙

Dark-first design system

One accent color, flat surfaces, hairline borders, editorial typography with tabular numerals, haptics on key actions, and adaptive navigation that goes bottom-bar on phones and side-rail on desktop.

📍

Background GPS

A foreground service keeps long walks recording while you use other apps or pocket the phone.

🇮🇳

Built for real food

Roti, dal, paneer, momos, biryani — the catalog covers Indian staples alongside western basics.

🐧

Android + Linux

One Flutter codebase, two native targets. Phone in your pocket, desktop on your desk.

🔒

Migration-safe data

Versioned database migrations mean app updates never wipe your history. Weeks of training data, preserved across every upgrade.
